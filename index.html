about

Hands-on technical research translated into useful intelligence for defenders.

Stephen J. Hilt is a threat researcher specializing in industrial control systems, cybercrime investigations, and advanced persistent threats. His work uncovers how attackers target critical infrastructure and turns those findings into practical guidance for defenders.

Across nearly two decades in security, he has combined protocol analysis, reverse engineering, honeypot research, underground economy tracking, and field-tested assessments to connect deep technical evidence with the larger threat landscape.
