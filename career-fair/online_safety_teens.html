The internet is amazing, but it comes with risks. People online aren't always who they say they are. Once something is shared digitally, it can never truly be deleted. This guide will help you lock down your accounts and stay safe. Check each box as you complete the settings below!
🔒 LOCK DOWN YOUR APPS

SNAPCHAT SETTINGS

  • Set "Contact Me" to My Friends only
  • Set "View My Story" to My Friends only
  • Turn OFF "Quick Add" (prevents strangers finding you)
  • Set "See My Location" to Ghost Mode
  • Disable "See Me in Quick Add"
  • Turn ON "Two-Factor Authentication" in settings
  • Review your Friends list — remove anyone you don't know IRL
  • Turn OFF "Allow Others to Find Me Using My Phone Number"

TIKTOK SETTINGS

  • Switch to a Private Account (Settings → Privacy)
  • Set "Who Can Send You Messages" to No One or Friends
  • Set "Who Can Duet/Stitch With You" to Friends or Off
  • Turn OFF "Suggest Your Account to Others"
  • Set "Who Can Comment" to Friends
  • Enable "Restricted Mode" for content filtering
  • Turn OFF "Allow Your Videos to Be Downloaded"
  • Review "Connected Apps" and remove ones you don't use
🚨 RED FLAGS — TRUST YOUR GUT
  • Someone you only know online asks for photos, personal info, or to move to a different app
  • An online "friend" asks you to keep your conversations secret from parents or friends
  • Someone sends you inappropriate content you didn't ask for
  • Anyone threatens to share your photos/info unless you do what they say — this is a crime, tell an adult immediately
  • Someone says they're your age but their story doesn't add up
  • An account uses flattery, gifts, or special attention to build trust quickly
  • Pressure to meet up in person with someone you've only met online
💡 SMART HABITS
🛡 Never Share These

Full name, school, address, phone number, location, daily schedule, or anything that identifies where you are

📸 Think Before You Send

Never send photos or videos you wouldn't want your parents, teachers, or future employers to see. Screenshots are forever.

🔒 Password Power

Use different passwords for each app. Use a password manager. Turn on two-factor authentication everywhere.

💬 Tell Someone

If something online makes you uncomfortable, tell a trusted adult. You won't get in trouble — they want to help you stay safe.
